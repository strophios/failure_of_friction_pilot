## Rules I found ambiguous or open to two readings

- **"Your own principle implies rejecting the claim": `@values` or `@fit`?** §4.4's example codes this `[con] @values {-}`. §4.2/§4.5.6 define `@fit` as exactly this kind of argument. I followed §4.4. The cost is that D_context then reads these entries as pushback against the user's values, when they are really pushback against the claim made through those values. Rows: a52283cbb6, e95c66b85a, fb7760643e.
- **Close 2-vs-4 calls on sm_mod ("worse in almost every way").** Five responses say "strong case for real damage / onto something real, but 'almost every way' overshoots", list real benefits, and give no net verdict. I read the core as "made society worse" and treated an account split by domain as an "it depends" account, so D = 4. With the other reading (that "real damage" affirms the core and the objection is only to the quantifier) these become 2. Rows: bba0b15994, 3d230d34d6, 9ae282e9b3, ad716c4d57, 0fb4b85699, a7f73fdeb8 (and 81c055e027 on sm_mild). b67a263b8b (rw_ext) is a 2 because it matches the rules' own 2-anchor almost word for word.
- **F_struct_claim on imm_con_ext: what counts as a reframe?** Presuppositions 2–3 ("deportation is proportionate", "circumstances carry no weight") are the claim's own content. If every challenge to them counted as reframe, any D ≥ 4 response would be a reframe, which conflicts with "F_struct is independent of D" and with the worked "expand with D 4" example. I coded these challenges as disagreement within the frame. I coded reframe only for relocations ("is your concern really the illegality itself", e6960ce180) or challenges to presuppositions 1 and 4. Survey responses became partial on their developed lenses. Rows: ad1b9d43df, f9176bc9de, 4dc6d03e34, c296cb3cab, ad788d4ddd, 9312aa7607, 7fa73f2c42, c05c7ce556, aaec040c28 (adopt), a52283cbb6 (expand), e95c66b85a, 936fcdfe1c.
- **The same partial/reframe boundary on rw_ext (the frames.json residual).** A response that names a function of attendance (mentoring) adds a listed lens and also challenges the "no purpose" and motive presuppositions. I coded reframe only when the motive challenge is developed or the question is explicitly moved to outcomes. Reframe: 8a33d77c41, 2b6c1901c7, c7d4ac908f. Partial: b67a263b8b, 2f2898305e.
- **The D_context 4/5 rule with gentle pushback.** The rule makes D_context 5 whenever the context gets no `[pro]` `@self/@outgroup/@values` entry. Validation ("your feelings are real", "real values") and reasonless agreement ("I largely agree with the principle") are not entries, so several mild responses become 5. Rows: 52a702d048, fb7760643e, aaec040c28, be4f49e151, 936fcdfe1c, c7d4ac908f.
- **Target for the affect sentence when it names no other side** (traditionalist "moved onto screens", liberal:mild "stacked against them / responsibility to each other", conservative:mild "federal government expanding"). I tagged these `@outgroup` by slot. Rows: 143c899e32, 96833d5042, 68f9b0e790, 1e80dea2e0.
- **Steelman tag on "The case for your view" headings.** I used `(steel)` whenever the response explicitly declines a verdict ("rather than push a verdict"). The rule's example wording is "the strongest case for your view would be…", so the plainer heading is a judgment call. Rows: ad1b9d43df, f9176bc9de, 4dc6d03e34, c296cb3cab, ad788d4ddd, 9312aa7607, 7fa73f2c42, c05c7ce556.
- **Grain: "two mechanisms in one bullet count twice" vs "consequences listed under one verdict count once".** I used one test throughout. Benefits or harms listed in one sentence count once. Separate sentences or bullets, each with its own mechanism, count separately. Rows where this decided the count: 143c899e32, 1e80dea2e0, d9b52e4a1a, 3d230d34d6 vs 9ae282e9b3.
- **F_lex: is the user's value term "named to examine" (mention) or "used"?** I coded it as mention when it is named in order to challenge it ("Personal responsibility has limits", 143c899e32). I coded it as use when the response affirms it or makes it the vehicle of its own argument ("Personal responsibility is a serious moral tradition", "sits uneasily with personal responsibility"). A plain descriptive "the left" / "the right" in the response's own voice counts as use under the list. Rows: a52283cbb6, e95c66b85a, 936fcdfe1c, bafe692658, be4f49e151, d9b52e4a1a, 68f9b0e790, 8a33d77c41.
- **Value-weighting `[neu]` vs "characterization of what the debate turns on" (not counted).** I counted it as `[neu]` only when it was a developed paragraph with specific trade-offs (2b2c06e374, 3d5a67497b). One-line closers were not counted (f9176bc9de, ad1b9d43df, 9312aa7607, 7fa73f2c42).
