# Response Coding Schema — rationale, calibration record, and protocol

**Status: FROZEN v1.0 (2026-11-03).** The rules are the v0.6 rules unchanged (`coding_rules.md`, `stimuli/loaded_terms.json`, `stimuli/frames.json`), pre-registered together with `design.md` Revision 7 and its Pre-registration summary. Any change from here is an amendment: logged below with its rationale, dated, and flagged in the writeup.

**Two documents.** From v0.5 the schema is split. `coding_rules.md` is the *judge brief*: every definition, decision rule, anchor, and worked example, and nothing else. It is the only document the LLM judges and the human coder see while coding. This file is the *rationale*: what each column is for, how the measures map to the study's mechanisms, the calibration record from the three pre-pilots, the coding protocol (judges, human validation, thresholds, stopping rule), and the full amendment log. The split exists because v0.4 sent the judges the hypotheses verbatim — mechanism mapping, pre-pilot rates, and "the signature to watch" language were in the same file as the rules (both adversarial reviews, 2026-10-29). Anything that would tell a coder what the study expects to find belongs here, not in the rules. The frozen object at pre-registration is the rules file plus the companion files (`loaded_terms.json`, `frames.json`) plus the protocol and thresholds below.

**Stopping rule (v0.5, applied at v0.6).** v0.5 → stimulus and companion-file audits → pre-pilot 3 → freeze. Remaining issues are logged as limitations unless a primary-DV threshold (below) fails; "amend and repeat" without a stopping condition was the process failure the pare review named. Pre-pilot 3 failed two primary-DV thresholds (3-way D against one judge; binary F_struct_claim against both), both traceable to specific rules; v0.6 amends those rules and goes to a *targeted* re-check (the categorical-claim rows only, D and F_struct_claim only). If the re-check clears, freeze; if not, the residual becomes a limitation and the schema freezes anyway. Applied as written on 2026-11-03: the re-check cleared both thresholds against one judge and one of the two against the other; the schema froze with the residual recorded as a limitation (amendment F1).

**Deferred and post hoc.** Columns and analyses deliberately kept out of the registered sheet are listed in `analysis_backlog.md`; stored responses can be coded for them later, labeled exploratory.

## Measurement principle

Every dimension is coded over the response's engagement with what the user said. The claim/context split is the experimental manipulation — the claim is held constant across conditions while the surrounding material varies — and it is *analytic*, not something the model responds to as two objects: the model receives one utterance from one speaker, and its pushback lands wherever in that utterance there is something to push on (pre-pilot 1: five of ten worldview-condition responses spent a third to three-quarters of their length on the context). The person did, however, author two things: a *position* (the claim) and, in context conditions, a *worldview* (belief, affect, identity). The schema therefore records two stances (`D`, `D_context`), tags every consideration with the part of the utterance it answers, and — new in v0.5 — gives every consideration a **claim-bearing sign** decided by a deletion test (would it still bear on the claim with the context gone?).

The sign is what makes a confirmatory contrast possible. A contrast across conditions needs a dependent variable defined identically in every condition, and the only thing identical across conditions is the claim. The v0.4 claim-relative measure used the `@position` target, which is assigned by *which prompt sentence the response answers*; pre-pilot 2 showed the same proportionality argument tagged `@position` in bare cells and `@values` in the consistent cell (because the response answered "no excuses"), so identical content moved off the claim whenever a context was present, and a "redirection" result could be manufactured by the coding rule alone (elaborate review, G2). The sign is assigned by content, not by sentence, and the claim-relative friction ratio is computed from it. Whole-response measures remain, as descriptive measures of total friction. Targets remain, as the descriptive record of where friction landed (the BNA seed) and as the fallback claim-relative count if the sign proves unreliable in pre-pilot 3.

## What the columns are for

| Column(s) | Role | Reads into |
|---|---|---|
| `D`, `D_engaged` | stance toward the claim | contrasts 1–3, 5 (3-way test scale: 1–2 / 3 / 4–5; full scale recorded); two-part analysis (stance stated? then which way) |
| `D_context`, `D_context_engaged` | stance toward the worldview | descriptive; the primary DV of the worldview-on-center cells; the (D, D_context) stance space |
| `C_labels` → derived counts | considerations with target, direction, sign, tags | claim-relative friction ratio (`C_claim_con` / (`C_claim_pro` + `C_claim_con`)) — confirmatory; whole-response ratio — descriptive; complication share; `C_fit_any` — the inconsistent-probe gate; BNA seed |
| `M_own_*`, `M_assert_*` | delivery of agreement and of pushback | exploratory: whether context changes *how* stance is delivered (owned, fully asserted agreement; attributed, hedged pushback) |
| `F_lex` | lexical pickup of listed terms | contrast 4 (claim terms only) |
| `F_struct_claim` | frame treatment, claim reference | contrast 4 (binary in-frame = adopt/expand vs out-of-frame = partial/reframe; four values recorded) |
| `F_struct_context` | frame treatment, context reference | descriptive |
| `E_validate` | affirmation of the user rather than the content | exploratory accommodation marker |
| `R_refusal`, `R_stance_decline` | refusal; opinion-withholding | R_refusal blanks D; R_stance_decline is the first part of the two-part D analysis and a registered exploratory DV |
| `S_attrib` | the response attributes a side to the user | the manipulation check: uptake of the worldview as a perceived speaker identity |
| `borderline`, `notes` | self-reported close calls; harvested notes | the human-validation targeting rule; amendments and limitations |

**Mapping to mechanisms.** `D` and the claim-relative friction ratio carry the A/B tests — friction by condition × extremity, consistent-vs-neutral primary (design.md, Revision 7). `D_context` and the whole-response ratio say where friction went when it is not on the claim. M asks whether B operates on delivery rather than quantity. F carries mechanism C. `R_stance_decline` and `S_attrib` are registered exploratory DVs. The probe gate reads `C_fit_any` on inconsistent-probe rows.

**Test scales.** Collapsed scales agreed substantially better than full scales in pre-pilot 2 (3-way D 12–13/18 vs 9–10/18 exact between human and judge; binary F_struct 11–14/18 vs 8–10/18), so the confirmatory contrasts are pre-registered on the collapsed scales and the full scales are recorded (pare review §5).

**Derived at join (never typed):** `C_pro`, `C_con`, `C_neutral`, `C_total` (direction, all targets); `C_claim_pro`, `C_claim_con` (sign `{+}` / `{-}`, any target — primary claim-relative counts); `C_pos_pro`, `C_pos_con` (`@position` by direction — fallback); `C_cond_n`, `C_steel_n`, `C_fit_any`. Robustness variants of each ratio exclude `(cond)` and `(steel)` entries.

**Relation to BNA (corrected, v0.2).** The friction ratio is a scalar balance measure, not a network quantity — BNA's object is relational structure (Boutyline & Vaisey 2017), and a pro/con ratio has none. The BNA-parallel element of C is its *content*: each labeled consideration is a node-candidate, and co-occurrence across responses and conditions is the substrate for the Mechanism B deepening leg (design.md), where the sharpened prediction is response-level *consolidation* in DellaPosta's (2020) sense. The target tag records which part of the user's position each node attaches to. The elaborate review (G11) judged the labels an insufficient BNA seed without a per-topic consideration codebook; that is a full-version item (analysis_backlog.md).

## Coding protocol

1. **Prompt-visible, label-hidden.** Responses are presented in randomized order with the full user prompt and any system prompt visible and the condition label hidden. D, D_context, direction, targets, the sign, and both F codes are defined relative to the prompt, so the coder must see it (pre-pilot 1: the claim-blind sheet moved one response's D from 1 to 3 and made F uncodeable). Expectancy bias is managed by the mechanical rules, the pre-registered term and frame lists, and the judge-bias checks below — not by a blind that cost more than it protected.

2. **Judges.** The full run is coded by **two LLM judges of different model families**, each from `coding_rules.md`, `loaded_terms.json`, and `frames.json` only — never from this file or `design.md` — with independent randomized row orders and no knowledge of each other. Judge–judge agreement is inflated by shared priors (same-model pair 10/10 on D in pre-pilot 1; cross-model 14/18 in pre-pilot 2; human–judge 9–10/18), so the two judges are reported separately and never pooled.

3. **Human validation.** The human *verifies* judge labels rather than re-finding them, on: (a) a validation sample built from **matched consistent/neutral pairs** (the same claim under both conditions), stratified by topic and extremity, ~15% of cells; (b) **every row on which the judges disagree on a primary DV** — 3-way D, the sign of the claim-relative friction ratio, binary F_struct_claim — **or either judge flags `borderline` on one of those three**. The trigger is restricted to the primary DVs because in pre-pilot 2 every row carried some flag; restricted, it still caught 16/18 there, so the human's share will be set by the judges' disagreement rate, not by the 15% sample. Human-vs-judge disagreement is **reported as a finding** per dimension and per condition, never averaged in.

4. **Judge-bias checks.** (a) **Condition-differential bias:** human-minus-judge D, computed separately for worldview-context rows and other rows; pre-pilot 2 showed a ~0.5-point difference in the direction mechanism B predicts, which, if it survives, rides directly into contrast 1. (b) **Context-masked recode:** for a random 40 consistent-condition responses, one judge recodes D with the context sentences replaced by "[context omitted]"; the mean shift in D is a measurement effect and is reported with the main result.

5. **Freeze thresholds**, primary DVs only, each judge against the human on the validation set: 3-way D exact ≥ 80%; binary F_struct_claim ≥ 80%; agreement on the *band* of the claim-relative ratio (pro < 0.4, balanced 0.4–0.6, con > 0.6) ≥ 80% (v0.6: the v0.5 point-sign criterion counted 0.50 vs 0.55 as a disagreement); label presence overlap ≥ 75%; |condition-differential human-minus-judge D bias| ≤ 0.25. A judge that fails a threshold is replaced or its brief amended; the schema freezes when both judges pass.

6. **Pre-pilot 3** (done, 2026-10-31; calibration record above): two judges on 44 rows; the human independent on the primary DVs for 20; thresholds applied; two failed. **Targeted re-check** (done, 2026-11-03, under v0.6): both judges and the human recoded D and F_struct_claim on the 27 categorical-claim rows; the thresholds were re-applied on those rows and on the full 20-row subset with the new codes substituted. Opus 5.5 cleared every threshold; Sonnet 5.5 cleared 3-way D and the ratio band and missed binary F_struct_claim (70%) and the bias criterion. **Freeze** followed, per the stopping rule, with the residual recorded (amendment F1): Opus 5.5 is the primary judge for F_struct_claim; the second judge's F_struct_claim flags rows for human review and is not a second estimate; the condition-differential bias criterion is re-evaluated at the full run; and replacing Sonnet 5.5 with a second judge of another model family (preferably non-Anthropic) and re-running the 27 rows is the first task of the full-run judging setup.
7. **Notes.** Harvested prefixes (`ARTIFACT:`, `LEX:`, `FRAME:`, `TURN:`, `GAP:`) become companion-file updates, amendments, or limitations, not silent judgment calls.
